What these measurements establish

Source metrics are recorded for every imported rollout snapshot. The operation observer can follow new completed imports with a shared observation protocol. Its outcomes describe individual operations. Semantic metrics appear when a saved Behavior map is measured; they are not yet an automatic semantic pass over every rollout. Older producer revisions stay stored but are excluded from the current rollout list.

Exploration, deviation, recovery and Plan completion remain unavailable until their required work-history and result evidence is validated. Unavailable measurements are never plotted as zero.
