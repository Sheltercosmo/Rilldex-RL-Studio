### One investigation, from signal to a checked action

1. **Notice a change.** Overview leads with evaluation/task outcomes, raw versus
   shaped reward, behavior changes, and evidence readiness. Retain native
   training/system metrics as context, with relevant subsets expanded by the
   question and recorded recipe. Configuration and observer changes appear on
   the same timeline. Avoid an undifferentiated wall of charts.
2. **Define a valid comparison.** Brush the changed range or select an affected
   population. Carry its experiment, branch, generating policy, learner uses,
   task/harness/evaluator versions, sampling settings and observation protocol.
   State what intentionally varies. A policy comparison must not accidentally
   match away the policy change it is meant to measure.
3. **Test competing explanations.** Within the existing investigation show
   evidence for/against incomplete observation, changed population, changed
   reward/evaluator, changed learner input, and changed behavior. Each finding
   names its scope, missing evidence and next distinguishing check. Descriptive
   contributions and correlations are not causal probabilities.
4. **Inspect comparable cases.** Offer matched tasks, affected slices,
   representative cases and counterexamples. Open the existing behavior map
   focused on the relevant sequence (feedback, response, changed attempt,
   subsequent check), with expansion into all units. Preserve the comparison on
   return. A few readable cases do not establish population prevalence.
5. **Plan and verify a response.** Reuse Checks & findings for a fixed-task
   evaluation, scorer replay, sync-control run, instrumentation repair or bounded
   configuration experiment. Record the expected distinguishing result and cost
   before execution. Bring returned evidence into the same investigation and
   compare it to the pinned baseline. Do not automatically change a trainer.

For closure, distinguish **membership known**, **generation ended**, **scoring
settled**, **observations available**, and **learner uses recorded**. An open
stream can close bounded generation windows without ending the training job.
Late corrections produce new versions; they do not change frozen findings.
Binary outcome bounds may be shown only when a fixed population and outcome
definition justify them; they are bounds, not predictions of pending outcomes.

For comparison, show both observed change and a fixed-task/explicitly weighted
comparison when support allows it, with lost overlap and unknown cases visible.
Inspect output length as a possible behavior/mechanism; do not automatically
control for a post-generation variable when estimating the overall policy
change. Do not infer task difficulty from length. A response that is short
because it aborted must not be treated as an easy problem.

### Validation should test decisions, not only components

Use three complete researcher journeys: outcome deterioration; reward growth
without independently measured improvement; and increasing effort without
improvement. Vary the underlying mechanism: completion order, missing scores,
changed task mix, evaluator changes, learner filtering/policy lag, actual behavior
regression, and mixtures of these. Include both apparent improvements and drops,
no-change controls, missing instrumentation and a genuine regression that should
not be dismissed as lag. Do not author a detector knowing the injected answer.

Measure correct interpretation, unsupported causal conclusions, missed real
regressions, time to relevant evidence, time to a useful next check, annotation
correction impact, and observation cost. Use independently reviewed cases and a
native trainer capture before claiming real-job utility. The long synthetic
simulation and the fresh tau2 trial exercise different boundaries; together they
still do not establish this complete workflow or distributed RL accuracy.

Defer extra map types, a larger tag catalog, personality labels, generic anomaly
scores and more isolated provider demonstrations unless one of these journeys
requires them. Keep the unit/work map central as inspectable behavioral evidence.

### Research reviewed for this priority change

- [AReaL diagnosis](https://areal-ai.io/docs/en/best_practices/algo_perf.html)
  distinguishes evaluation, rollout and consumed-trajectory rewards, including
  changes introduced by filtering. Its debugging recipes motivate concrete
  follow-up checks, not universal hyperparameter recommendations.
- [AReaL metrics](https://areal-ai.io/docs/en/reference/metrics_tracking.html)
  documents independent asynchronous workflow logging and masked batch metrics;
  denominators and reduction units must accompany metric names.
- [verl fully async training](https://verl.readthedocs.io/en/latest/advance/fully_async.html)
  documents processing-order effects, staleness, partial rollouts and multiple
  synchronization modes. "Async" alone is not enough to choose a diagnostic.
- [PACE, September 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36830v1)
  separates lag accumulated during generation from lag in the completed pool.
  Its abstract supplies a design motivation; its reported gains are not locally
  reproduced and are not acceptance evidence for this product.
- [NeMo PPO](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/rl/nightly/guides/ppo.html)
  exposes critic/return and train-generation mismatch measures alongside shared
  metrics; a group-relative-only dashboard would omit relevant evidence.
- [NeMo length penalties](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/rl/nightly/guides/length-penalty.html)
  separates environment reward from adjusted reward and records their difference.
  That is a concrete reason to preserve the reward transformation chain.

The current metric schema is `rollout-behavior/5`. The operation observer adds response inspection coverage, failed-operation fraction, unknown-operation fraction and completed-call-after-failed-call count. Failure uses complete, consistent terminal call outcomes as its denominator; pending and unknown calls are excluded and remain inspectable. These are operation observations, not task success or causal recovery. Five selected same-target transitions are present across the reviewed sources. [Definitions and evidence](BEHAVIOR_MAP_INTERFACE.md#scoped-operation-outcomes-and-repeated-work).

The Plan-stage observer adds observed stage changes, stage re-entries, product-response inspection coverage and the fraction of stages with recorded contents. Stage changes follow saved action/stage correspondence; re-entry means correspondence returns to a previously observed stage within the same Plan. Neither establishes mental replanning, progress or waste. Product-response coverage excludes preparations and includes explicit unresolved decisions. Contents are deduplicated by (Plan unit, stage) and do not establish completion. The software example has one stage change, zero re-entries, seven of seven product responses inspected, and contents recorded for one of three stages. Completion remains unavailable. Optional map projections do not create duplicate metric observations. [Definitions and evidence](BEHAVIOR_MAP_INTERFACE.md#plan-stages-and-observed-work).

The executor-report observer adds response-inspection coverage, error-report fraction, unknown error-observation fraction, warning-report fraction and target-revisit-after-error count. Fractions use **responses**, not tasks or Plan stages. Their denominator contains decidable observations; incomplete negatives and unresolved selections are excluded and reported separately. Coverage includes unresolved inspections and therefore is not accuracy. Missing observations remain unavailable, never zero. In the reviewed software rollout, 10/32 decidable responses report errors, while 1/33 responses has uninspected image material. Those are report statistics, not a 31.25% action failure rate. [Definitions and source review](BEHAVIOR_MAP_INTERFACE.md#executor-reports-on-the-same-cards-and-connections).

## Decision

Concentrate the next phase on proving useful, portable behavioral measurements. Interface and reliability work has progressed further than measurement validation. The current product is an evidence-oriented local research prototype, not a validated RL monitoring system.

Proposed positioning: **An environment-aware behavioral measurement layer that shows how an agent's way of working changes across training, with enough evidence and measurement controls to trust the comparison.**

Primary workflow: an RL researcher compares checkpoints on the same tasks, locates a meaningful behavior change, checks the underlying trajectories, and decides which training or environment hypothesis to test next. The product does not establish that an RL update caused the difference or automatically prescribe a training intervention.

The hypothesis worth testing is that reusable, validated measurement packs reduce the work between a changed outcome curve and an evidence-backed behavioral finding. More tags, trace viewers, classifiers, confidence values, or Harbor support alone do not establish differentiation. Jev's cost, speed and measurement quality advantages remain unmeasured in our workflow.

## General observations: five stable views

Fine-grained labels are supporting records. The researcher-facing framework should stay small and should survive changes to implementation details. These are proposed metric contracts and refinements, not claims that every measure below is already implemented or validated.

| Macro question | Defensible observation and unit | Research use | Interpretation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| How does it allocate and sequence work? | Per-rollout action-purpose shares and eligible within-agent transitions among explore, apply, verify, coordinate, report, unresolved; later, reviewed episode boundaries | Locate a strategy change and retrieve contrasting examples | More exploration or checking is not inherently better; action share is not token/time share |
| How does it persist or update after feedback? | Exact recurrence plus a separate response distribution among linked feedback opportunities; distinguish repeat, parameter change, method change, information seeking and continuation | Inspect whether a changed outcome accompanies different responses to evidence | Repetition can be warranted; a changed next action does not prove learning or effective adaptation |
| What objective is it pursuing? | Recorded root objective, authorized amendments, expressed subgoals and action-to-candidate associations; switches/returns only through observed intervals | Locate changes in focus, deferred work and visible departures from instructions | Subgoal switching is not objective drift; missing candidate identity is unknown; intention and attainment require separate evidence |
| How does it check and close work? | Check attempt -> linked result -> subsequent action or submission; separate observable checks, self-reported completion and verifier outcome | Inspect changes in verification practice or stopping | A command named `test` or a passed output line does not prove a claim is supported; artifact/version correspondence is required for stronger assertions |
| What outcomes and resources accompany these behaviors? | Separately sourced task outcomes, reward, termination, duration and scoped token/cost records, joined by rollout/task/checkpoint | Prioritize changes with practical consequences and inspect task-specific tradeoffs | Reward, task success, reported completion and progress are distinct; association does not identify a causal mechanism |

Each metric contract must contain: researcher question; observable definition; unit; eligible opportunities and denominator; evidence requirements; exclusions; unknown/not-applicable states; extraction/harness versions; validation set; uncertainty; intended follow-up; and conditions under which comparison is disallowed. A metric without a credible interpretation or a research action belongs in an exploratory view, not the default monitor.

Example: a method-change measure should divide confirmed method changes by all eligible linked-feedback opportunities, retaining unresolved cases and displaying coverage. A decided-only rate may be a secondary statistic but must not silently become the population rate. If there are p confirmed positives and u unresolved cases among N opportunities, [p/N, (p+u)/N] is a missingness bound assuming the decided labels are correct; it is not a confidence interval and does not cover classifier error.

There are two time axes. Within a rollout, show episodes and exact recorded order. Across training, compare fixed macro measures at recorded policy checkpoints on a defined task cohort. Do not turn fine-tag frequencies into a training story without checking task mix, budgets, sampling, coverage and measurement-version changes. Keep the five macro views stable; version the detailed mapping and bridge old/new mappings on the same frozen traces.

## Jev and general extraction architecture

Recommended separation:

1. **Producer adapters** preserve instructions, actions, observations, actors, tool IDs, order, timestamps, outcomes and resources. They report explicit, inferred and absent fields; they do not decide whether behavior is good.
2. **Environment evidence packs** declare task criteria, tools, constraints, feedback meanings and observable state/artifact markers, with provenance and availability time. New environments add this evidence rather than rewriting every metric.
3. **Candidate builders and retrieval** supply objective candidates, relevant previous attempts, matching artifacts/checks and a prefix-bounded context window. They report unsupported candidates, missing spans and truncation. Extraction may use rules or a separate LLM, with its cost and errors recorded.
4. **Jev measurement packs** ask small, explicit decisions about supplied evidence and alternatives. Independent questions sharing evidence can be batched. A question depending on an earlier result needs a later request with that result in its state. Code composes answers into episodes and metrics.
5. **Analysis and presentation** join versioned measurements to cohorts/outcomes and expose macro changes, coverage, task contributions and original evidence. Export the same records for Scout, notebooks and existing experiment trackers.

This fits [TypeSafe's documented primitives](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives). Its returned confidence summarizes an option distribution; it does not replace our validation ([confidence documentation](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence)). Current code implements up to five questions, gated by evidence requirements, with at most three preceding actions. It preserves nearby harness context and feedback origin, but lacks richer environment projections in the packet. That is a bounded initial harness, not a general solution for long trajectories.

Jev cannot select a correct subgoal that was never offered, and a confident selection cannot recover omitted evidence. Score candidate availability and retrieval sufficiency separately from decision accuracy. Route unfamiliar/ambiguous cases to richer retrieval, an optional LLM or review; do not allow generated descriptions to silently become validated monitoring categories. Specialized risk/failure diagnosis remains a later layer.

Each adapter should expose a capability manifest. The first shared version now reports action/result correspondence, ambiguous batches, missing context, objectives, policy identity and support for the three initial contracts. Artifact versions remain unrecorded. Enable each metric only when its evidence requirements are met. A parsed file alone does not demonstrate framework support. A portability test should run the same measurement contract on real Harbor and SWE traces without editing its semantics; the current format and gating tests are an initial step, not semantic transfer validation.

## Accuracy has five separate failure points

| Layer | What to evaluate |
| --- | --- |
| Extraction | Source-event recall, field correctness, call/result association, ordering, duplicate suppression, revisions and missing modalities |
| Evidence/candidates | Whether the required evidence and a correct selectable alternative are present; invalid or overlapping alternatives; context sufficiency on long trajectories |
| Decisions | Per-question confusion, precision/recall and abstention coverage; performance by task/producer; agreement and adjudicated reviewer disagreement |
| Aggregates | Error in rollout/task rates and sequence summaries relative to reviewed data; false/missed shifts and direction agreement; sensitivity to coverage and weighting |
| Longitudinal operation | Same-inspector comparisons, stable sampling, policy provenance, performance on frozen anchors, and separation of model changes from inspector changes |

Optimize harness design on development data and report held-out results while keeping argmax selection fixed. Split by task/repository or other meaningful group before tuning; repeated actions from one trajectory must not populate both development and evaluation as if independent. Keep intent/purpose judgments blind to later outcomes. Stronger-model labels are comparison measurements or reference proposals, not human ground truth.

An action classifier can perform well while systematically mismeasuring checkpoint differences. Validation must therefore include macro-rate error and whether the claimed change survives reasonable measurement uncertainty. Existing paired task intervals do not include inspector error. Monitoring many metrics repeatedly also needs a prospective alert policy and false-alert evaluation before automatic alerts are claimed.

Three concrete lessons affect the product design:

- **Count the actual research unit.** The 128 episode slots repeat 91 game identities. Join by the recorded episode slot and game identity; split and resample by game. Task-description text alone is an even weaker key (89 distinct strings). Episode-weighted and equal-game-weighted success rates differ, so make the weighting visible.
- **Gate semantic measures on available evidence.** All 12,592 recorded steps have action text, validity, reward and terminal flags, but no native observation text. The agent's descriptions of feedback are not a substitute. These files support outcome, length, source-validity and conservatively extracted command measurements; rich feedback-response judgments require a different export. Every episode ends with `done`, including zero-reward episodes, so terminal status must remain separate from success.
- **Track extraction changes alongside behavior changes.** Adjacent identical commands among extractable pairs rise from 0.27% to 0.60% to 1.38%, but unknown pairs fall from 898/4,900 to 49/4,199 to 2/3,109. The missingness bounds overlap. This does not establish a rise in repetition across the full population, and repetition itself is not a failure category. A monitor that hides these denominators can tell the wrong training story.
